# Algorithmic Complexity

When analyzing sorting algorithms, we often look at asymptotic bounds like $O(n \log n)$. 

For a summation series, the formula is rendered as:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}
$$
